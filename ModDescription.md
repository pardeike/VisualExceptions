Visual Exceptions makes RimWorld errors easier to understand.

Instead of leaving you with only a wall of log text, it shows exceptions in a window and highlights the mods involved in the stack trace.

What it helps with:

- see repeated exceptions while playing
- copy enhanced stack traces
- suppress one noisy exception until restart
- open mod pages when available
- test-disable a mod or patch from the exception view

It does not magically know who is guilty, but it gives you much better clues.
